# What the competitors charge

**Status:** ℹ️ Reference, 27 Oct 2026. The product owner's question: how much Wix, Squarespace, Pinegrow, RapidWeaver Elements, Hostinger, Jimdo, Framer and the other builders compared so far charge, what their plans are and what each plan buys. This note records the answer next to Webforge's decided price ([MVP spec §8](../../spec/02-mvp-spec.md#8-business--licensing-model-decided-2026-10-19): one licence, US $99, unlimited sites, 7-day trial). It changes no decision; it is the reference the pricing page, the comparison on the website and WF-6's selling point draw on.

## 1. Method and limits

Prices are US list prices read on 27 Oct 2026, per month on annual billing unless stated, and before hosting, domain and tax. Official pricing pages were used where they could be read (Bootstrap Studio, Blocs, Pinegrow, Realmac, Mobirise); the hosted builders render prices by region and often show the monthly figure only at checkout, so theirs come from 2026 reviews that agree with each other, and the cells say where they disagree. Every vendor changes prices during the year (Webflow in May, Framer in June, Elements in June, Wix in August, Durable in March), so a figure is current to the month at best. Nothing here is a recommendation to copy another product's price list; it is the market Webforge's one number sits in.

## 5. What it says about Webforge's price

- **Every hosted builder costs more than Webforge within a year**, except Hostinger's prepaid intro, which climbs at renewal. The persona's line in the spec, "hates monthly builder fees", matches the market: the cheapest own-domain hosted plans are $10–19 a month, and the ones with a store $29–39.
- **Hosted pricing is getting more complex, not less.** Webflow and Framer bill seats and AI credits apart, Squarespace's transaction fees fall by tier, GoDaddy and Hostinger rely on the renewal uplift. One number with nothing else to read is itself a difference, as WF-6 records.
- **Among desktop apps Webforge sits in the middle.** Bootstrap Studio undercuts everyone at $59 for life, Blocs is $109–180 per major version, and Elements and Pinegrow have moved to $99–249 a year. Bootstrap Studio's bundled hosting and forms for its Lifetime price are the one thing in these tables Webforge doesn't match; Webforge's answer is that the site works on any host and the form services are the person's own (B3).
- **No desktop app has a free tier**; Mobirise's free app is funded by a $149 kit, and Pinegrow and Elements run trials. Webforge's 7-day trial is on the short side: Squarespace and Pinegrow give 14 days, and Elements limits the trial by pages rather than days. Whether to lengthen it is a decision for the licensing work after the MVP, not this note.
