# Version history in the other competitors

**Status:** ✅ Decided 2026-11-26: VH-1 to VH-3 adopted, VH-1 and VH-3's summary in Phase 5 and the rest in v1.1 ([§6](#6-decisions)). PG-10 to PG-13 stand as decided
**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Question:** [Pinegrow's Revisions](06-pinegrow-revisions.md) led to PG-10 to PG-13. What do the other competitors offer for going back to an earlier version, and does it change those decisions or add to them?

**Short answer:** No other product reviewed does what Pinegrow does, and most do less than Webforge does today. The four decisions hold: each has at least one other product doing it, and none is common. Three smaller ideas come out of the comparison: keeping an automatic version, copying a section out of a version, and reviewing changes before publishing.

## 1. Method

- **25 products** from the [list of competitors](00-index.md), read on 26 Nov 2026 from their own help pages, documentation, release notes and forums. Nothing was run.
- **Three groups:** desktop apps that export files (7), hosted builders for owners (11), and designers' builders with the tools an owner already knows (7). Onlook was looked at and has nothing documented beyond Git.
- **"Not found"** means no documentation was found for a point, not that the product lacks it. Several help centres refused to be read directly; where a fact comes from a search result's summary of an official page, or from a user on a forum, [§2](#2-what-each-product-offers) says so.
- **Pinegrow** is in [competitors/06](06-pinegrow-revisions.md) and is not repeated here.

**Confidence:** high for Webflow's backups, Figma, WordPress, Squarespace, Webnode and Publii. Medium for Wix, Framer, Google Sites, Bootstrap Studio and Sitely. Low to medium for Mobirise, Nicepage, Blocs, Elements, Durable and GoDaddy's Airo, whose pages say little. An absence is the weakest kind of finding here.

**IP rule (unchanged from UI1):** we may copy interaction patterns and layout conventions. We never copy another product's code, CSS, icons, images, help text or wording.

## 3. What the comparison shows

- **Webforge is already ahead of most.** Six of the eleven hosted builders for owners have no version history at all, and all seven desktop apps keep only copies of the whole project. Only Webflow and Figma document saving the current state before a restore, as Webforge does, and none restores as one step Undo takes back.
- **Saying what changed is rare, and never in a version list for owners.** Durable and Shopify Canvas list edits in words; both are editors where an AI makes the changes. WordPress 7.0 gives a summary for one page. Wix, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Webnode, Canva and every desktop app show a date.
- **A visual before and after is rarer still:** Pinegrow, Figma (one frame, for developers), WordPress 7.0 (one page) and Google Sites (before publishing). No hosted builder for owners has one.
- **Restoring part of a site takes two forms.** Google Sites restores one page; WordPress and Elementor keep revisions page by page. Framer, Figma and Bootstrap Studio let the owner copy a part out of an old version. Only Pinegrow restores an element in place.
- **Renaming is ordinary** (Wix, GoDaddy, Webflow, Google Sites, Figma, Publii). Figma and Webflow add a description; Wix adds a star.
- **Timers are common where there is no autosave to a single file:** Bootstrap Studio and Framer every 5 minutes, Figma every 30, Blocs on its own timer.

## 4. Do PG-10 to PG-13 hold?

| Decision                                 | Others doing it                                                          | Verdict                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PG-10** rename a version               | Wix, GoDaddy, Webflow, Google Sites, Figma, Publii                       | Holds. It is the one thing here where Webforge is behind the ordinary                                                                         |
| **PG-11** each version says what changed | Durable, Shopify Canvas, WordPress 7.0 (one page); Pinegrow counts files | Holds, and puts Webforge ahead of every builder for owners. Naming pages and parts, as decided, is more than any of them do in a version list |
| **PG-12** before and after for one page  | Pinegrow, Figma, WordPress 7.0, Google Sites                             | Holds for v1.1. Two patterns to weigh in its plan: WordPress's marks beside the scrollbar, and Figma's one-over-the-other slider              |
| **PG-13** restore one page               | Google Sites; WordPress and Elementor page by page; asked for at Wix     | Holds. Google Sites brings back a deleted page too, as PG-13 does                                                                             |
| **PG-14** restore a section in place     | Pinegrow only                                                            | Still not built. Copying from a version (VH-2) is how everyone else answers it                                                                |
| **PG-15** versions on a timer            | Bootstrap Studio, Blocs, Framer, Figma                                   | Still not built. Those timers guard against losing work, which autosave does in Webforge; its versions are for going back, and are named      |

## 5. Proposals

| ID   | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Seen in                                                  | Why                                                                                                                                                                                      | Size                                             |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| VH-1 | **Keep an automatic version.** _Keep This Version_ in the menu of a version Webforge saved: it is no longer deleted with the oldest, and no longer counts towards the 20                                                               | Wix's star; Figma naming an automatic version            | Only the last 20 automatic versions are kept. An owner who finds the right "Start of the day" can protect it today only with _Open as New Site…_. Renaming alone (PG-10) doesn't keep it | 0.5 day; fits with PG-10 in Phase 5              |
| VH-2 | **Copy a section from a version.** With a version's page open in the comparison (PG-12), _Copy Section_ puts one of its sections on the clipboard, to paste into the page as any copied section is                                     | Framer, Figma, Bootstrap Studio                          | It gives most of PG-14 without its questions: the owner chooses where the section goes, and nothing is replaced unasked                                                                  | Not sized: with PG-12's plan, v1.1               |
| VH-3 | **Review changes before publishing.** _Review Changes_ in Publish opens PG-12's comparison on the newest _Published_ version, and PG-11's summary sits beside the Publish button ("Home and Contact changed since you last published") | Google Sites; Pinegrow's compare with what was published | Publishing is when an owner most needs to know what is about to go live. The summary costs little once PG-11 exists                                                                      | Summary 0.5 day after PG-11; the rest with PG-12 |

Not proposed:

- **A description as well as a name** (Figma, Webflow): a name is enough for one owner.
- **A log of who changed what** (Webflow, Wix, WordPress.com): Webforge has one owner at one computer, and Undo History names each step.
- **A list of changed layers with their old and new values** (Figma's Dev Mode): for developers.
