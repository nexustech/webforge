# What competitors' users complain about and ask for

**Status:** ✅ Decided 2026-11-26: UC-2 to UC-5 adopted, UC-2 and UC-5 in Phase 5 and UC-3 and UC-4 in v1.1; UC-1 decided, the number of computers on 27 Nov 2026 as SL-1 ([§6](#6-decisions))
**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Question:** What do the users of other website builders ask for, complain about and leave over, and which of those gaps does Webforge close, share or leave open?

**Short answer:** Most complaints are about the business, not the editor: prices that change, renewals, support, and sites that can't be taken away. Webforge's design answers the largest of them, and should say so. It shares three gaps with every desktop rival: someone else updating the site from another computer, forms that need a third party, and consent for scripts the owner adds. Five proposals follow, three of them decisions with no building.

## 1. Method

- **27 products** in three groups, read on 26 Nov 2026: eight desktop apps (Bootstrap Studio, Pinegrow, RapidWeaver Elements, Blocs, Mobirise, Nicepage, Sitely, Publii), eleven hosted builders for owners (Wix, Squarespace, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Webnode, Jimdo, Durable, Carrd, Google Sites, Canva Websites, Square Online and Weebly), and eight others (Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Elementor, Shopify's themes, and the AI site generators Lovable, Bolt and v0).
- **Sources:** each vendor's forum and request board, GitHub issues, Trustpilot, independent reviews, and news of price changes. Nothing was run.
- **What could not be read:** Reddit, at all. G2 and Capterra, except through search summaries. Vote counts, except on Webflow's wishlist, Framer's board and GitHub. Mobirise's threads (titles and counts only) and Nicepage's wishlist.
- **Bias to keep in mind:** Trustpilot gathers billing and support grievances; Webflow and Framer score about 1.5 there and about 4.4 on G2. One study of 600 one-star reviews, quoted below, was made by a competitor of the builders it studies.
- **Webforge's side** comes from the MVP spec, the help pages and ADR 0012, not from the running app.

**Confidence:** high for Blocs, Elements and Webflow's requests, where vendors' own boards were read. Medium for Wix, Squarespace, Bootstrap Studio, Framer and the AI generators. Low for Nicepage, Mobirise, Google Sites, Canva and GoDaddy. "Why people left" is thin everywhere: mostly intentions, and articles by firms that sell the move.

### 2.2 Four findings worth more than their row

- **A desktop app is Webflow's most-voted request:** 5,418 votes, answered only as "Reviewed". It is the largest single number found, for the thing Webforge is.
- **Anger follows surprise, not price.** People write about a renewal they didn't expect far more than about what a builder costs. Blocs and Bootstrap Studio are praised for one thing above all: no subscription, and nothing moved behind a new tier.
- **Being locked in matters when leaving matters.** "Can't export" is 8% of the one-star sample; it is raised after a price rise or a shutdown. Ownership sells as protection against those, more than as a feature.
- **The AI generators' users pay to fix the tool's mistakes.** The top complaint for all three is credits spent on errors the tool made, and a small change costing a prompt.

## 4. What follows

- **The offer is the answer to the top four complaints**, and nothing built by Webforge says so yet. A price that doesn't move, a site you hold, and work that can't be lost are worth more words on the website and in the app than any feature.
- **The licence's open questions are product questions now.** "Updates for a year, then renew" would bring Webforge the complaint its rivals are praised for avoiding.
- **Hand-off is the gap that most needs an answer.** It is the one thing users of every desktop builder ask for, and each vendor answers "later" or "a hosted service". Webforge can't edit in a browser without a server, but it can make passing the site to a second person, and back, a thing it supports.

## 5. Proposals

| ID   | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                        | Size                                                                 |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| UC-1 | **Settle the licence's three questions with these complaints in mind**, and write the answers as promises: what the price includes and for how long, that nothing in the app moves to an add-on or a tier, how many computers, and that a site already made opens, exports and publishes whatever happens to the licence or to us | Themes 1, 3, 10 and 14. It is the complaint users write about most, and the cheapest to answer                                             | A decision; no building until 6.0a                                   |
| UC-2 | **A copy before a site changes format.** The first time a newer version of the app saves a site, it keeps the file as it was beside it, named for the version; an older version of the app opening a newer site says so in plain words and changes nothing                                                                        | Theme 6. Bootstrap Studio's and Blocs' users ask for exactly this, and neither vendor will promise it. To check first: what happens today  | About 1 day, once checked                                            |
| UC-3 | **Added scripts can wait for consent.** A script in Custom Code gets **Wait for the cookie banner**, as Google Analytics does; Site Check says when a site loads something from elsewhere without asking                                                                                                                          | Theme 13. Sitely's users ask for it; ours can add a chat or a pixel today that loads before the banner is answered                         | About 1.5 days; v1.1                                                 |
| UC-4 | **Passing a site to someone else, and back.** One licence covers a second person on their own computer; **Send a Copy to Edit…** saves the site as a ZIP that opens in Content mode with the latch on (PG-18), and opening the returned copy shows what changed (PG-11) before it replaces the owner's                            | Theme 8. It needs no server, uses what is already adopted, and answers the seat complaint at Webflow and Framer                            | About 3 days after PG-11 and PG-18; v1.1. The licence part is UC-1's |
| UC-5 | **Say what Webforge is against these complaints**, in the app's first screen, the help and the website: your files, one price, nothing lost, AI only if you turn it on. A help page for people leaving Wix or Squarespace, on bringing their words and pictures across                                                            | Themes 1, 4, 5 and 7, and the 5,418 votes. Importing a site stays out of scope, so the page is the honest answer to "can I bring my site?" | About 1 day of writing                                               |

Not proposed:

- **Editing in a browser, and our own form relay.** Both need a server and accounts (M5, B2). The evidence says they are wanted; it doesn't change what they cost.
- **Importing a site from another builder.** Wix lists it as a request, and none of them offers it. It stays out of scope.
- **Linux.** Asked of Mobirise and Publii, by few.
- **A public board of requests with votes.** The vendors that have one are criticised for not answering it.
