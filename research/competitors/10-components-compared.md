# Sections and elements: what competitors offer, and Webforge's gaps

**Status:** ✅ Decided 2026-11-27: CP-1 to CP-4 adopted for Phase 5, CP-5 and CP-8 for v1.1; CP-6 and CP-7 not adopted ([§6](#6-decisions)). CP-9 to CP-17, the settings of ten common elements, all adopted: CP-9, CP-10, CP-12 and CP-14 for Phase 5, the rest for v1.1 ([§7.4](#74-decisions))
**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Question:** Which sections and elements do other website builders offer, how far do the common ones go (a gallery, testimonials, pricing, an FAQ, a contact form), and what is Webforge missing?

**Short answer:** Webforge has every piece that nearly all of them have, and more ready-made business sections than most. Its gaps are narrow: the Gallery has three layouts and no settings, forms have six kinds of field and no upload, and four elements that many builders have are missing (a quote, an audio player, a pop-up, a scrolling strip of logos). Eight proposals follow; the first four are small. A second pass over the settings of ten common elements ([§7](#7-settings-of-ten-common-elements)) finds Webforge level on text, pictures, buttons and the Header's behaviour, and thin on the Accordion, Tabs, the Carousel and Columns; nine small proposals follow from it.

## 1. Method

- **26 products** read on 27 Nov 2026 from their help pages, element references, feature pages and, for Shopify's themes, their source: eleven hosted builders for owners (Wix's classic Editor and Studio, Squarespace, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Webnode, Jimdo, Durable, Carrd, Google Sites, Canva Websites, Shopify's Dawn and Horizon themes), eight desktop apps (Bootstrap Studio, Pinegrow, RapidWeaver Elements, Blocs, Mobirise, Nicepage, Sitely, Publii), four builders for designers and WordPress (Webflow, Framer, WordPress 7.1's own blocks, Elementor), and three component libraries as a reference for what is ordinary on the web (daisyUI, Flowbite, Bootstrap 5.3).
- **Nothing was run.** A product that publishes no full list (Bootstrap Studio, Blocs, Mobirise, Wix, GoDaddy, Canva) can only be marked "not found" for what its pages don't mention, never "absent". Framer publishes no list of what its insert menu holds, so it is left out of the counts below.
- **Webforge's side** is `docs/help/sections.md`, `docs/help/elements.md` and the catalogue in `packages/site-components/src/catalogue` on `main` of 27 Nov 2026.
- **A second pass, the same day,** read the settings of ten common elements in 13 of the products ([§7](#7-settings-of-ten-common-elements)), and Webforge's in `docs/help` and the catalogue.
- **Sizes** in [§5](#5-proposals) and [§7.3](#73-proposals) are first guesses from reading the catalogue. Each item needs its own `Implementation plan`.

**Confidence:** high for WordPress, Squarespace's blocks, Elements, Sitely, Publii, Durable's and Webnode's section lists, and the three libraries. Medium for Wix, Hostinger, Nicepage, Elementor, Webflow and Carrd. Low for Bootstrap Studio, Blocs, Mobirise, GoDaddy, Jimdo, Canva and Framer.

**IP rule (unchanged from UI1):** we may copy interaction patterns and layout conventions. We never copy another product's code, CSS, icons, images, help text or wording.

## 2. What nearly everyone has, and what few have

**In nearly every product:** text with headings, an image, a button, a gallery, a slideshow or carousel, a video, a lightbox, an FAQ or accordion, a contact form, a map, social links, a place for custom code, and a header and footer. Webforge has all of them.

## 3. The five common ones in depth

### 3.1 Gallery

| Option                      | Webforge today                                                               | Others                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Layouts                     | _Grid_ (squares), _Natural shapes_, _First image large_                      | Wix: eight, with collage, thumbnails, slider and slideshow. Squarespace: six. Sitely: grid, masonry, bento. Elementor Pro: grid, justified rows, masonry |
| How many across             | Fixed: two, three, then four as the page widens                              | WordPress 1 to 8; Elements 1 to 12; Publii 1 to 8; Elementor up to 24; Squarespace, Hostinger and Webnode have a setting                                 |
| One shape for every picture | Squares in _Grid_, with no choice                                            | WordPress: one ratio and a crop switch. Elements: square, wide or tall. Elementor Pro: six ratios. Webnode: square, portrait or none                     |
| Space between pictures      | Fixed                                                                        | Hostinger, Elements, Mobirise                                                                                                                            |
| A link on a picture         | No: a click always opens it larger                                           | Wix, Squarespace, Sitely, Elementor Pro, WordPress                                                                                                       |
| Slideshow or thumbnails     | The separate Carousel element                                                | Wix, Squarespace, Sitely, Nicepage as layouts of the gallery                                                                                             |
| Video among the pictures    | No                                                                           | Wix, Webflow, Sitely, Elements                                                                                                                           |
| A filter by category        | No                                                                           | Elementor Pro, Nicepage, Blocs                                                                                                                           |
| Opening larger              | Previous and next, arrow keys, swipe, captions; a plain link without scripts | The same in most. Zoom in Wix; zoom, and swiping to close, in Elements' paid add-on. Squarespace shows no captions there                                 |
| Limits                      | None stated                                                                  | Squarespace and GoDaddy: 250 pictures                                                                                                                    |

### 3.2 Testimonials

- **Webforge:** three layouts (_Cards_, _Large quotes_, _Photo beside each quote_), each with a quote, a name, who they are and a photo; and a Carousel layout for them.
- **Others:** Squarespace has a list, a carousel and a slideshow; Webflow six layouts; Durable a rotating quote and a section fed from Google's reviews. Elements, Sitely, Publii, Hostinger, Jimdo, Carrd, Google Sites and WordPress have no testimonials piece.
- **Stars:** Elementor, Shopify's Horizon, daisyUI and Flowbite have a star rating to show. Webforge has none.
- **Reviews from Google:** Durable, and apps for Wix. It needs a request to another site, which Webforge's exported sites don't make.

### 3.3 Pricing

- **Webforge:** _Cards_ or _Price list_; a plan has a name, a price, what it's per, what's included and a button; one plan can be highlighted, with a badge.
- **Others:** Wix's and Squarespace's are for selling memberships, not a table of prices. Durable, Webnode, Nicepage, Pinegrow and Mobirise have a pricing section whose options aren't documented. Elementor Pro has a price table with a ribbon.
- **No gap found.** Nobody documents a switch between monthly and yearly, or a table that compares plans feature by feature.

### 3.4 FAQ

- **Webforge:** _Questions that open_ (working without scripts), _Two columns_, _All answers shown_.
- **Others:** one open at a time or several is a setting in Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Elements, Bootstrap Studio and Bootstrap. Wix adds a search box and categories.
- **Data for search engines:** only Elementor and Jimdo offer it. Webforge decided against it (SEO-11), on Google's own guidance, and that stands.

### 3.5 Contact form

| Option                  | Webforge today                                                                                     | Others                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kinds of field          | Six: a line of text, an email address, a phone number, a message, a tick box, a choice from a list | Squarespace 20; Nicepage about 20; Elementor Pro 17; Framer 11 with date, time and number; Hostinger four                                                                          |
| A date or a number      | No                                                                                                 | Squarespace, Framer, Elementor Pro, Elements, Nicepage, Mobirise's paid builder                                                                                                    |
| A file from the visitor | No                                                                                                 | Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy, Webnode, Jimdo and Carrd, mostly on paid plans; Bootstrap Studio, Elements, Nicepage, Sitely; Webflow's upper plans                                     |
| Protection from spam    | A hidden field and a timing check                                                                  | reCAPTCHA in most; Turnstile or hCaptcha in Carrd and Elements; Sitely and Framer do without a puzzle, as Webforge does                                                            |
| Where messages go       | A form service the owner chooses                                                                   | Their own servers, for hosted builders. A PHP script on the host (Elements, Blocs, Sitely) or the vendor's relay (Bootstrap Studio, Mobirise at 10 a day, Nicepage on a paid plan) |
| Steps and conditions    | No                                                                                                 | Wix, Nicepage, Elementor Pro; follow-up questions in Squarespace                                                                                                                   |

## 4. Other pieces Webforge lacks

| Piece                                                                   | Who has it                                                                                            | Note                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **An audio player**                                                     | Wix, Squarespace, Jimdo, Carrd, WordPress, Elements, Blocs, Nicepage, Sitely                          | Needs Media to take sound files                                                                              |
| **A pop-up**                                                            | Wix, Elementor Pro, Bootstrap Studio, Elements, Blocs, Nicepage, Sitely; a paid extension in Mobirise | Sitely opens one on a click, after a time, on leaving or on scrolling                                        |

| **Logos that scroll**                                                   | Wix, Squarespace, Hostinger, Shopify's Horizon, Bootstrap Studio 9, Framer; a paid add-on in Elements | Movement that never stops needs a way to pause it, and must stand still for visitors who ask for less motion |
| **Breadcrumbs**                                                         | WordPress, Bootstrap Studio, Elements, Nicepage, the three libraries                                  | Webforge's pages are flat, apart from the blog, so there is little to show                                   |

| **A feed from Instagram**                                               | Squarespace, Hostinger, Durable, Sitely                                                               | Needs requests to another site, which exported sites don't make without consent                              |

Musicians, podcasts, guided practices. Media must take sound files first                                                    | About 2 days                            |
| CP-8 | **A pop-up, decided on its own.** A dialog opened by a button (a booking form, a menu's details), never by itself. A pop-up nobody asked for is among the most disliked things on the web, and the announcement bar already carries a message | About 3 days for the button-opened kind |

Not proposed:

- **Logos that scroll.** Common, but it is movement for its own sake; the Logos section's row and grid do the job. Look again if owners ask.
  - **Looked at again, 27 Nov 2026:** a band of scrolling text, not logos, came out of the One Page Love research for a playful theme (#1575). The product owner chose to build it in v1.1 ("Build it in v1.1"), over a still band made from a Section with one line: a **Scrolling text** section that a visitor can pause, that stands still for visitors who ask for less motion and on the canvas while it is edited, and that needs no request ([#1573](https://github.com/nexustech/webforge/issues/1573), about 2 days, not yet a plan item). Logos that scroll stay not proposed.
- **A gallery filter, and video among its pictures.** Three and four products. Wait for a portfolio collection, where a filter belongs.
- **Slideshow and thumbnails as gallery layouts.** The Carousel element is the same thing; say so in the Gallery's help.
- **Breadcrumbs, a table of contents, before and after pictures, a QR code.** Rare, or of little use on a flat site.
- **Reviews from Google, and a feed from Instagram.** Both need the visitor's browser to call another site.
- **Steps and conditions in forms.** For a form builder, which Webforge isn't.

## 7. Settings of ten common elements

The sections above ask which pieces exist. This one asks what an owner can set on the ten that nearly every product has: text, a picture, a button, a list, columns, an accordion, tabs, a carousel, the header and a form.

**Method.** The settings pages of 13 products, read on 27 Nov 2026: Wix (the classic Editor and Studio), Squarespace 7.1, Hostinger, Webnode, GoDaddy, WordPress 7.1's own blocks, Elementor, Elements, Sitely, Nicepage, Bootstrap Studio and Blocs. Nothing was run. Webflow's help refused every request and is left out. Bootstrap Studio, Blocs, GoDaddy and Webnode document few settings, so "who has it" below counts only products whose pages say so; the true numbers are higher. Elementor's pages don't say which settings need Pro. Webforge's side is `docs/help/elements.md`, `styling.md`, `header-and-footer.md` and `forms.md`, checked against the fields in `packages/site-components/src/catalogue`.

### 7.2 Where Webforge is ahead

- **Each device its own,** on nearly every option of the Style tab. Most hosted builders document it for a few settings; three document a text size for each device.
- **A carousel that pauses,** and stands still for a visitor who asks for less motion. None of the six settings pages mentions reduced motion, and only Sitely documents a way to build a pause button.
- **An accordion with no scripts,** and tabs that answer the arrow keys.
- **The Header on scrolling:** five styles. Wix and Sitely document as many; Squarespace has one.
- **Spam checks with nothing to solve.** Sitely is the only other; the rest set up reCAPTCHA or a like service.
- **Alt text for each place a picture is used,** which no page describes.

Together about 5 days. CP-9, CP-10, CP-12 and CP-14 are the ones nearly every product has.

Not proposed:

- **The owner's own words after a form is sent.** The site's own page at `thank-you` is used when there is one; say so in the Forms help before building a setting.
- **Example text in a field, help under it, two fields in a row.** Two products each. Example text in place of a label is a known accessibility fault.
- **Filters on a picture, a shadow or outline on text, a drop cap, text fitted to the width.** Decoration with little call from a small business; look again if owners ask.
- **Hiding something on one device.** Six have it, but what is hidden on a phone is still downloaded and still read out, and pages drift apart. **Don't publish** and _One under another_ cover most of the need.
- **A button as wide as its column, and a background for one column.** Two and four products. Look again if owners ask.
- **Several slides in view, thumbnails, tabs down the side, a menu of panels, a chosen folding width.** For a designer's tool.
- **Data for search engines on an accordion.** SEO-11 stands.
