# Every competitor compared so far

**Status:** ℹ️ Reference, 20 Nov 2026. A list of every product the research notes have compared Webforge with, and where each comparison is. It decides nothing; the decisions stay in the notes it links to.

**Date:** 2026-11-20

**Question:** Which competitors has the research compared Webforge with so far, and in which notes?

## 1. Method

All the notes under `research/` on `main` of 20 Nov 2026 (commit `966982c0`) were searched for product names, and each comparison table and "what others do" section was read. A product is listed when a note compares Webforge with it: in a table, a review or a "what others do" section. A name mentioned once in passing is left out, and so are services a site links to (Mailchimp, Stripe, the booking tools in [product/05](../product/05-feature-gap-analysis.md)) and the hosts in [stack/08](../stack/08-publishing.md).

Short names in the tables:

- **competitors/01–05:** [Webflow](01-webflow.md), [Pinegrow](02-pinegrow.md), [the November 2026 comparison](03-comparison-2026-11.md), [Onlook](04-onlook.md), [Webnode](05-webnode.md).
- **product/01–21:**

  - [06 editor UI parity](../product/06-editor-ui-ux-parity.md), [07 look-and-feel pass](../product/07-look-and-feel-pass.md), [08 component styling](../product/08-component-styling.md), [08 start screen](../product/08-start-screen-comparison.md)
  - [09 quick start](../product/09-quick-start-wizard.md), [10 pricing](../product/10-competitor-pricing.md), [12 search visibility](../product/12-search-visibility.md), [13 Phase 3 feedback](../product/13-phase-3-feedback.md)

## 9. Limits

- Most hosted builders were read from their help pages and reviews, not run; each note says how its own comparison was made.
- Only Bootstrap Studio, Webflow, Pinegrow, Onlook and Webnode have a review of their own. The others appear in comparison tables, so what is known about them is limited to those tables' topics.
- Keep this list current: a note that compares a new product adds a row here, and a new review in this folder adds its link.
