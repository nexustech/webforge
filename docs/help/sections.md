# Sections

A **section** is a full-width band of a page: a Hero at the top, a Gallery, a Contact form and so on. Sections stack one under another. Inside each one are **elements** such as headings, text, images and buttons (see [Elements](elements.md)).

To add a section, open the **Add** panel's **Sections** tab and click one: it goes after the selected section. You can also drag it onto the page, or click the **+** between two sections on the page. See [The Add panel](add-panel.md).

## What every section has

Select a section and open the Edit panel's **Style** tab. Its options come first, then its look, background before text, then its spacing:

- **Layout:** most sections come in two or more layouts, listed below.
- **Colours:** _Page colours_, or _Reversed_, which swaps the theme's light and dark colours to make a dark band on a light page (or a light band on a dark one).
- **Content width:** _Narrow_, _Medium_, _Wide_ or _Full_. The background always fills the page's width; this sets how wide the content inside is. On a computer, the content of sections at the same width lines up, a Hero's text and image too, and so do the Header and Footer at that width. On a phone, a Hero keeps a little more space at its sides.
- **Space inside** and **Space outside:** the room inside the section's edges (padding) and above and below it (margin).
- **Background image** (in the Content tab, on every section but the Hero, which has _Image behind the text_ with the same Overlay settings), with an **Overlay** (_None_ to _Very strong_) and **Overlay colour** that darken or lighten the picture so text stays readable. {appName} warns you when the text would be hard to read. A gradient from **Style This…** is drawn over the picture and under the overlay; its **Gradient opacity** sets how much of the picture shows through (50% unless you change it).
- **Entrance animation:** _Fade in_, _Slide up_, _Slide in from the left_, _Slide in from the right_ or _Zoom in_, with a _Delay_. It plays once, when a visitor scrolls to the section, and never for visitors whose device asks for less motion.
- **Appearance:** background colour, corners, border, shadow and opacity, then its text. See [Styling](styling.md).

Many sections hold a list of items, such as a Gallery's images or an FAQ's questions. Add, remove and reorder them in the Edit panel's **Content** tab. While the section is selected, the Add panel's **Suggested** group offers its kind of item too.

## Hero

The large opening section of a page: a title, a short text, a button and an image.

- **Layouts:** _Image beside the text_, _Centred, image below_, _Image behind the text_, _Text only_.
- **Alignment:** _Centre_ or _Left_.
- **Text on the picture**, in _Image behind the text_: _Middle_ or _Bottom_, with the **Alignment** you chose. At the bottom, the picture's subject stays in view above the title, text and button. It shows most on a tall Hero.
- **Overlay** and **Overlay colour**, in _Image behind the text_ once it has a picture: how strongly a layer of colour covers the picture (_None_ to _Very strong_), in the theme's _Neutral_ colour or the _Page background_, so the title and text stay readable. The text takes the colour that suits the overlay's. A new Hero starts at _Strong_, in _Neutral_. A Hero made before you could choose keeps the overlay it had, shown as _As before_; once you choose another, _As before_ isn't offered again. {appName} warns you when the text would be hard to read.

## Text

A title and formatted text: paragraphs, bold, links and lists.

## Text + Image

Text beside a picture. **Layouts:** _Image on the right_ or _Image on the left_.

## Call to action

Asks visitors to do one thing, with a title, a short text and a button. **Layouts:** _Banner_, centred in the theme's dark colour (or in the reversed colours when its _Colours_ are _Reversed_), or _Button beside the text_.

## Features

What you offer, or the reasons to choose you. Each **Feature** has an icon, a title and a text.

- **Layouts:** _Cards_, _Centred icons_ or _List_. A last row that isn't full is centred, as in Testimonials, Team, Pricing, Stats and Latest posts: four features in rows of three put the fourth in the middle under them.
- **Cards when hovered** sets what the cards do under the pointer (see [Styling](styling.md#hover-effects)).

## Gallery

A set of pictures, each with an optional caption.

- **Layouts:** _Grid_, _Natural shapes_ (each picture keeps its own shape) or _First image large_.
- On your site, clicking a picture opens it larger, with previous and next buttons; visitors can also use the arrow keys or swipe.

## Testimonials

What your customers say: a quote, their name, who they are and an optional photo. **Layouts:** _Cards_, _Large quotes_ or _Photo beside each quote_.

## Team

The people behind the business: a photo, a name, what they do and a short text. **Layouts:** _Cards_, _Round photos_ or _List_.

## Pricing

Your plans or a price list. Each **Plan** has a name, a price, what it's per ("a month", "a session"), what's included and a button.

- **Layouts:** _Cards_ or _Price list_. As cards, one or two plans are centred, each as wide as one of three.
- Turn on **Highlight this plan** to show one plan in your brand colour. Only a highlighted plan shows its **Badge** ("Most popular").
- For a café's or a restaurant's dishes and drinks, or any list of prices without buttons, use the [Food and drink menu](#food-and-drink-menu).

## Food and drink menu

Your menu, or any list of prices: each **Dish or drink** has a name and a price, joined by a dotted line, with a **Description** under them. A new menu comes with breakfast and drinks to change.

- **Groups:** give a dish a **Group heading** ("Starters", "Drinks") to start a group above it. The dishes after it are in that group until the next one with a heading.
- **No price:** leave the **Price** empty for a dish that has none ("Ask at the counter"). It then has no dotted line.
- On a phone, a long name wraps, with the dotted line and the price on its last line.

## FAQ

Questions and answers. A new FAQ comes with three sample questions to change, such as _When are you open?_ **Layouts:**

- _Questions that open:_ visitors click a question to see its answer. This works even when the visitor's browser has scripts turned off.
- _Two columns._
- _All answers shown._

## Stats

A row of numbers you're proud of ("25 years in business"). Each **Stat** is a number and what it counts. The numbers are shown as you type them.

**Layouts:** _Row_, _Cards_ or _Beside the text_.

## Steps

How it works, in a few steps: "Get in touch", "Get a quote", "We get to work". Each **Step** has a title, some text and a **Date**. The steps are numbered for you, in their order, and read out as a numbered list by screen readers. Side by side on a computer or tablet, one under another on a phone.

**Layouts:** _Numbered_, or _Timeline_, which shows each step's date ("Week 1", "March") along a line instead of its number.

## Logos

"As seen in", your customers or your partners: a row of logos, each with an optional link. **Layouts:** _Row_ or _Grid of tiles_.

## Video

A YouTube or Vimeo video with a title and text. **Layouts:** _Text above_ or _Text beside_. Paste the video's address from YouTube or Vimeo into **Video address**; other addresses show no video.

Your site shows a placeholder with a _Play the video_ button first. Nothing loads from YouTube or Vimeo until the visitor clicks it, or accepts all in the cookie banner, which keeps your site fast and private. YouTube videos use its privacy-enhanced player.

## Map

A map of your address. **Layouts:** _Text above_ or _Text beside_. Type the address, or show the one from [Site Settings](site-settings.md#contact-details-and-opening-hours). Like a video, the map loads only when a visitor clicks _Show the map_, or accepts all in the cookie banner. Your site uses Google Maps for an address, or OpenStreetMap when you paste an OpenStreetMap link.

## Opening hours and contact

Your opening hours, address, phone number and email, taken from [Site Settings](site-settings.md#contact-details-and-opening-hours). Change them there, once, and every page that shows them updates.

- Turn **Show opening hours** and **Show contact details** on or off.
- Anything left empty in Site Settings is left out.
- **Layouts:** _Side by side_ or _One above the other_.
- It can go in a column of the [Footer](header-and-footer.md#header-and-footer) too, where it shows without its background and spacing, its title as the column's. There, its **Style** tab offers only what shows in the column: **Show opening hours** and **Show contact details**.

## Contact form

A ready-made form asking for a name, an email address, a message and, if you turn on _Ask for a phone number_, a phone number. **Layouts:** _Text above_ or _Text beside_.

Messages are sent to the form service you choose in Site Settings. Until you choose one, the form's button is turned off and the page reminds you. See [Forms](forms.md).

## Newsletter signup

An email signup for a newsletter service you already use. {appName} doesn't include one of its own. Paste the service's form address (starting `https://`) into **Form address**, and the name it expects for the email field into **Email field's name**. Turn on **Ask for a name** if your service takes one.

This section sends sign-ups straight to that service, not to the form service in Site Settings.

It can go in a column of the [Footer](header-and-footer.md#header-and-footer) too, where it shows without its background and spacing, its title as the column's. There, it has no layout, colours, spacing or entrance to set: the column draws none of them.

## Section

A blank band to fill with any elements you like. **Space between elements** sets how far apart they sit.

## Contact buttons

Round **Call**, **WhatsApp** and **Email** buttons that stay at the bottom left of the screen while visitors scroll, so they can get in touch from anywhere on the page. Adding them from the Add panel puts them after the Footer, so they're on every page; on the canvas they show there, and on your site they stay in the corner. They use the phone number and email address in Site Settings › Contact, unless you type others in the Edit panel. Turn each button on or off; **WhatsApp** is off until you turn it on, and needs the number with its country code (+44 7700 900123). A button with no number or address isn't shown. Each is a plain link: your site loads nothing from anyone until a visitor taps one.
