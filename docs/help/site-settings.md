# Site Settings

**Site Settings** hold the details your whole site shares. Change them here, once, and every page that shows them updates. Open them with **File › Site Settings…**.

They're in the same **Settings** window as {appName}'s own preferences, under **This site**. Click **Save** to keep your changes, as one step you can undo.

## General

- **Site name:** shown in the Header, in browser tabs and in search results.
- **Description:** a sentence or two about your business. Search engines show it for any page without a description of its own.
- **Language:** the language your site is written in, as a code such as `en`, `en-GB` or `de`. Spelling checks use it too.
- **Site icon:** the small picture in browser tabs and bookmarks. Choose a square picture from Media.
- **Show a back-to-top button:** a button that appears once visitors scroll down. It isn't shown in the editor.
- **Business type** and **More precisely:** what kind of business this is, such as _Restaurant_, _Plumber_ or _Vehicle repair_. Search engines use it.
- **Help AI tools read your site:** adds a plain-text copy of each page to your website, for AI tools that read them. Search engines don't read them. Off unless you turn it on.

## Web address

- **Web address:** where your site will be, such as `https://www.example.com`. Search engines and sharing need it. Without it your site has no sitemap and no picture when it's shared.

## Social & sharing

- **Social profiles:** your pages on Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, GitHub or elsewhere. The **Social links** element shows them.
- **Share image:** the picture shown when someone shares a page from your site. Each page can choose its own.
- **X username**.

## Contact details and opening hours

Shown under **Contact & hours**.

- **Business name**, **Email address**, **Phone number** and **Address**.
- **Opening hours:** add a row for each set of days, with the time you open and close.
- **Note on opening hours:** such as "Closed on bank holidays".
- **Price range:** as people would say it, such as "£" or "£10–£30".
- **Latitude** and **Longitude:** where you are on a map. In most map apps, press and hold your place to see these two numbers.

The **Opening hours and contact** section and the **Map** show these details, and so can headings and text. When a detail is empty, the editor shows a reminder to add it here.

{appName} also writes these details into your home page in a form search engines read, so they can show your hours and address. It only writes the details your pages show.

## Search engines

- **Let search engines show the site:** turn it off while the site isn't ready to be found.
- **Include a sitemap for search engines:** a list of your pages that helps search engines find them. Needs the web address.
- **Google verification code** and **Bing verification code:** paste the code from Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools, to prove the site is yours. Nothing is sent from {appName}.

## Analytics

- **Google Analytics measurement ID:** such as `G-ABC123DEF4`, to count your visitors. Leave it empty for no analytics.
- **Ask visitors before counting them (cookie banner):** shows a banner with _Accept all_ and _Decline_. Google Analytics only starts if a visitor accepts. With the banner off, visits are still counted, but without cookies and in less detail.
- **Banner text:** your own wording for the banner. Accepting also loads videos and maps.

With Google Analytics on, **Add Page › Cookie Policy Page** writes a page about your site's cookies for you to check. See [Terms and cookie pages](pages.md#terms-and-cookie-pages).
