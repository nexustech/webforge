# Media

**Media**, in the right column, holds your site's pictures and documents. They're kept inside your site's file, so they travel with it.

## Adding files

- Click **Add images or files…**, or drag files from your computer onto the Media panel.
- Paste a picture onto the page and it's added to Media.
- In an image option, **Add a new image…** adds one and places it straight away.

{appName} takes:
- **pictures:** JPEG and PNG;
- **documents:** PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, plain text and CSV.

Each file can be up to 50 MB, and each picture up to 50 megapixels. A file gets a web-friendly name from its file name, such as `shop-front`.

## A picture's details

Select a picture in Media to see:
- **Alt text:** a short description for people who can't see the picture, such as "Fresh bread on the counter". It's used everywhere the picture is placed, and search engines read it too. [Site Check](site-check.md) lists pictures without it.
- **Focal point:** click the part of the picture that must stay in view. When a shape or a small screen cuts the picture to fit, that part stays.
- **Crop:** *Original*, *Square*, *4:3*, *3:2*, *16:9* or *3:4*, centred on the focal point.
- **Replace…:** swaps in a new file everywhere the picture is used. Its name, alt text and focal point stay.
- **Delete:** says how many pages use it first. You can undo it.

## Placing a picture

Image options in the Edit panel have **Choose an image…**. Double-click a picture, or press Enter, to place it. **Alt text here** gives this one place its own description; leave it empty to use the one from Media.

## Documents

Documents show their type and size, such as "Word document, 820 KB". To offer one on your site, [link to it](links.md) with the **File** kind of link. Only documents something links to go on your website.

## On your website

When you export, {appName} makes each picture in modern sizes for phones, tablets and computers. Each has a copy that older browsers can show. Pictures load as visitors scroll to them, so pages open quickly. Pictures and documents that no page uses are left out.
