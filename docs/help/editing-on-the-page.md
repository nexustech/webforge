# Editing on the page

## Selecting

- **Click** a section or element to select it. A blue outline and a small bar of buttons appear on it, and the [Edit panel](edit-panel.md) shows its options.
- **Ctrl+click** (Cmd+click on a Mac) or **Shift+click** adds an item to the selection, or takes it out. With several items selected you can delete, duplicate, move them, or keep them off your website, together.
- The line above the page shows where the selection sits, such as _Page › Hero › Button_. Click any step to select it.
- From the keyboard:
  - **↑** and **↓** select the previous or next item.
  - **Ctrl+↑** selects what contains the item, and **Ctrl+↓** selects the first thing inside it.
  - **Ctrl+←** and **Ctrl+→** move between items side by side.

## The bar on the selected item

| Button                         | What it does                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Select the surrounding element | Selects what contains this item                                                                                         |
| Drag to reorder                | Drag it somewhere else on the page                                                                                      |
| Move up · Move down            | Moves it before or after its neighbour                                                                                  |
| Edit                           | Starts typing in its text, or chooses a new picture for an image                                                        |
| Style                          | Opens [Style this…](styling.md#style-this)                                                                              |
| Don't publish · Show           | Keeps it off your website without deleting it, dimmed here. To hide it only while you edit, use [Layers](layers.md)     |
| Duplicate                      | Places a copy just after it                                                                                             |
| Delete                         | Removes it. _Undo_ appears for a few seconds. On a Mac the key is Cmd+Delete; the delete key on its own clears the text |

## Changing text

Select a text item and press **Enter** (or click **Edit** on its bar), then type. **Backspace** on a selected item empties its text and starts typing; on an item with no text of its own, such as an image, Backspace deletes it.

While you type, a small toolbar offers:

- **Bold**, **Italic** and **Underline**;
- **Highlight** and **Text colour**, from your theme's colours;
- **Link…** and **Remove link** (see [Links](links.md));
- bulleted and numbered lists;
- alignment;
- **Rewrite…**, while the assistant is on: a friendlier or shorter version of the selected text, or its spelling and grammar fixed, for you to check first (see [Rewriting text](assistant.md#rewriting-text)).

Keys while typing:

- **Shift+Enter** starts a new line.
- **Alt+Enter** splits the text into two elements.
- **Shift+Space** types a space that never breaks across lines, for "10 am" or "£ 5".

Right-click while typing to see spelling suggestions.

## Adding

- **A section:** hover over a section and click the **+** beside it, then choose from the Add panel. Or use the [Add panel](add-panel.md) itself.
- **An element:** select a section, then click an element in the Add panel, or drag it into the section.

## Moving and copying

- **Drag** an item to move it. Hold **Ctrl** (Cmd on a Mac) while you drag to drop a copy instead.
- **Move up** and **Move down** are on the bar, and on Ctrl+Alt+↑ and Ctrl+Alt+↓. On Windows, Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ work too.
- **Cut**, **Copy** and **Paste** (Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) work on whole sections and elements, even between two of your sites.
- **Duplicate** (Ctrl+D) places a copy just after the item.

## Spacing and sizes

When a section or element is selected, drag the handles on its edges to change the room inside it (padding) and around it (margin). On an image, the side handles change its width. Each drag is one step you can undo.

## The right-click menu

Right-click a section or element, on the page or in [Layers](layers.md), for:

- **Rename** and **Duplicate**;
- **Save as shared section…**, or **Detach from shared section** (see [Header, Footer and shared sections](header-and-footer.md));
- **Select similar** (every item like this one on the page) and **Select parent**;
- **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste** and **Paste as Linked Copy**;
- **Style This…**, **Copy Style**, **Paste Style**, and **Apply Look to Every…** on this page or the whole site (see [Styling](styling.md));
- **Add Note…** or **Edit Note…**, and **Resolve Note** (see [Notes](#notes));
- **Help for This Element**, which opens its help page at what it says about that kind of section or element (see [The editor](the-editor.md#help));
- **Delete**.

On the keyboard, Shift+F10 or the menu key opens the same menu.

## Notes

A note is a reminder for you on a section or element, such as _Swap this photo before launch_. It's never on your website.

- **Add one:** right-click the item and choose **Add Note…**, or use Edit › Add Note…. Type the note and click **Save Note**.
- **Where it shows:** the item has a dashed amber outline on the page, and a note icon in [Layers](layers.md); point at the icon to read it. [Site Check](site-check.md) lists every open note under _Tips_.
- **Change or resolve it:** **Edit Note…** opens it again. **Resolve Note**, or emptying it and saving, removes it.

Adding, changing and resolving a note are each one step you can undo.

## Toolbars for some elements

A row under the canvas toolbar offers extra buttons for the selected item:

- **Heading:** its level, _Title_, _Heading_ or _Subheading_.
- **List:** **Add item** before or after the selected item, or at the end.
- **Columns:** **Add column**, **Remove last column**, the **Column widths** on the device you're viewing, and **Show column outlines**.
- **Header:** in the Mobile view, **Show mobile menu**, to see and edit the menu as it opens on a phone.
