# Styling

Most of your site's look comes from its [theme](themes.md): change a colour or a font there and every page follows. When one section or element should look different, change it in the Edit panel's **Style** tab. Your choices still come from the theme's colours, sizes and corners, so the site stays consistent and changing the theme later still works.

## Appearance

The **Appearance** group, at the top of the Style tab, is on every section and element except the Header and Footer. The blank Section has no text options.

- **Text:**
  - **Font:** _Default_, or the theme's _Headings_, _Body text_ or _Accent_ font.
  - **Text size**, **Weight**, **Italic**, **Underline**.
  - **Letter case:** _As typed_, _CAPITALS_, _lower case_ or _Each Word_.
  - **Text alignment**.
- **Colour:** **Text colour** and **Background**.
- **Box:** **Corners**, **Border**, **Border colour** and **Shadow**, and **Padding** on elements.
- **Opacity**.

_Theme's_ means the item follows the theme. Every option except Font can be [different on each device](edit-panel.md#different-on-each-device).

## Colours

Colour pickers show your theme's colours by their role: Brand, Accent, Second accent, Surface and Text, and Neutral for backgrounds. Some have lighter and darker **shades**, and you can make any colour see-through with **Opacity**. If a colour would be hard to read against what's behind it, the picker warns you. Icons and social logos need less contrast than text. On a picture, such as a section's background picture or a Hero's picture behind its text, it can't tell, so check that yourself.

In Advanced mode you can also choose a **Custom** colour. It stays the same when you change the theme.

A section's **Colours** option is _Page colours_ or _Reversed_. Reversed swaps the theme's light and dark colours, to make a dark band on a light page.

## Hover effects

**When hovered**, on buttons, Buy buttons, images and icons, sets what they do when a visitor points at them:

- _Lift_, _Grow_, _Tilt_, _Lift and shadow_, _Shadow_ or _Darken_, and _Underline_ on buttons.
- **Speed:** _Fast_, _Medium_ or _Slow_.
- **Movement:** _Smooth_, _Gentle stop_ or _Springy_.

Sections of cards (Features, Testimonials, Team and Pricing) have **Cards when hovered**. Nothing moves for visitors whose device asks for less motion.

## Entrance animation

**Entrance animation** sets how an item appears as visitors scroll to it: _Fade in_, _Slide up_, _Slide in from the left_, _Slide in from the right_ or _Zoom in_. Add a **Delay** to make items appear one after another. It plays once, and never for visitors whose device asks for less motion.

## Style this

**Style This…** opens the selected item's look in a window that fills {appName}'s window. Open it from:

- the button at the top of the Style tab;
- **Style** on the item's bar;
- the right-click menu or the Edit menu.

It offers more than the Style tab: gradients, a border on each side, each corner's rounding, inner shadows, a background when hovered, and the look of a section's title.

- **At the top**, previews show the item on Desktop and Mobile as you go. They stay in view while you scroll the settings.
- **To give the previews more room**, drag the line under them, or select it and use the arrow keys.
- **Below**, the settings fill the window's width, in as many columns as fit.
- **At the bottom**, **Where it applies**, **Cancel** and **Apply** are always in view.

**Where it applies** sets which items get the look:

- **This ‹item› only**
- **Every ‹item› on this page**
- **Every ‹item› on the site**
- **Make it the theme's look for ‹item›:** every item of this kind without a look of its own follows it, on every page, now and later.

Each choice says how many other items it changes. Click **Apply** to make the change; it's one step you can undo.

## Copy and paste a look

- **Copy Style** (Ctrl+Alt+C) and **Paste Style** (Ctrl+Alt+V) copy one item's Style settings onto another of the same kind.
- **Apply Look to Every ‹item› on This Page**, or **on the Site**, gives the selected item's look to every other item of its kind, in the Header, the Footer, shared sections and a [blog](blog.md)'s design too. {appName} asks first when other pages would change. The blog's design changes once, for every post.

These are on the right-click menu and the Edit menu. On a Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl.
