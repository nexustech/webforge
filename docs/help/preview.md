# Preview

**Preview** shows your site in your web browser, exactly as visitors will see it, with working links, menus, forms and animations.

## On this computer

Click **Preview** in the title bar, choose **File › Preview**, or press Ctrl+I (Cmd+I on a Mac) when you're not typing text. Your browser opens at the page you're editing.

The preview follows you as you work:

- change the theme or a style and it updates straight away;
- change a page and it reloads;
- go to another page in {appName} and the browser goes there too.

The preview works only on this computer, and only while {appName} is open. Nothing is put on the internet.

## Drafts and things you don't publish

The preview shows your website as it will be published, with one difference: it shows your [draft pages](pages.md#draft-pages) too, so you can check them before they go live. Open a draft from the page itself; the menu leaves it out, as your website's will.

Anything set to **Don't publish** in [Layers](layers.md#what-you-can-do) isn't in the preview, as it won't be on your website. Anything you **Hide while editing** is, as it is on your website.

## On your phone

Choose **File › Preview on Phone…**, then point your phone's camera at the code that appears. Or type the address shown into your phone's browser. Your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.

While it's on, the title bar shows **Phone preview on**, with a button to stop it. Anyone on your Wi-Fi who has the address can see the preview, so stop it when you're done. It also stops by itself after 10 minutes without use, or if your computer leaves the Wi-Fi.

Phone preview needs a home or office Wi-Fi. It may not work on public Wi-Fi.

## If {appName} asks about code

If your site contains code that came from somewhere else, {appName} asks before the preview runs it. See [Custom Code](custom-code.md#code-from-somewhere-else).
