# Layers

**Layers**, under the Add panel, lists everything on the page in order: the Header, each section and the elements inside it, and the Footer. Selecting in Layers selects on the page, and the other way round. Pointing at a row outlines that item on the page.

On a [blog post](blog.md), Layers also lists the blog's design: its sections above the post's own, after the Header, and those below, before the Footer. Renaming, hiding or adding a note to one of them changes the design, so every post shows it. Each change is one step you can undo, such as _Renamed Heading to 'Date' in the design of 'Blog'_.

It's the easiest way to reach something small or hidden, or to see how a page is built.

## What the icons mean

- **Link:** a linked copy of a [shared section](header-and-footer.md#shared-sections). On the Header and Footer, it means they're on every page; on a blog's design, on every post.
- **Lock:** the item can't be moved or deleted, only changed: the blog design's own sections.
- **Sparkles:** the item has an entrance animation.
- **Crossed-out name:** the item isn't published. It's dimmed on the page.
- **Name in italics:** the item is hidden while you edit. It isn't on the page in the editor, but it's on your website.
- **Note:** the item has a [note](editing-on-the-page.md#notes). Point at the icon to read it.

## What you can do

- **Select:** click a row. Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on a Mac) adds a row to the selection; Shift+click selects a run of rows.
- **Reorder:** drag a row up or down. Places it can't go are dimmed, with a line saying why. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) while dragging to drop a copy, or Ctrl+Shift to place a linked copy of a shared section.
- **Show or hide:** click the eye on a row and choose:
  - **Show:** on the page and on your website.
  - **Hide while editing:** out of your way in the editor, still on your website. Use it for something that covers what you're working on, such as a full-width banner.
  - **Don't publish:** stays in your site's file, dimmed on the page, but isn't on your website. Not publishing the Header or Footer leaves it off this page only.
- **Rename:** double-click a name, or press F2. Names are only for you; visitors never see them.
- **Open and close:** the arrow beside a name, or **Expand all** and **Collapse all**.
- **Filter:** type in the box at the bottom to find items by name or kind. Esc clears it.

The toolbar at the bottom has **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate**, **Don't publish**, **Rename** and **Delete**. Right-click a row for the same menu as on the page.

## Keys in Layers

| Keys                                      | What they do                                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| ↑ ↓                                       | Previous or next row                         |
| → ←                                       | Open or close a row                          |
| Ctrl+↑ · Ctrl+↓                           | What contains it · the first thing inside it |
| Ctrl+← · Ctrl+→                           | The previous or next item beside it          |
| Ctrl+Alt+↑ · Ctrl+Alt+↓, or Alt+↑ · Alt+↓ | Move it up or down                           |
| F2                                        | Rename                                       |
| Delete or Backspace                       | Delete                                       |

On a Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl.
