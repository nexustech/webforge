# The Add panel

The **Add** panel, the first tab of the left column, lists everything you can put on a page. It has two tabs:
- **Sections:** full-width bands such as a Hero or a Gallery. See [Sections](sections.md).
- **Elements:** what goes inside a section, such as a Heading, an Image or a Button. See [Elements](elements.md).

## Adding something

- **Click** a section to add it after the selected section.
- **Click** an element to add it to the selected section.
- Or **drag** either one onto the page, to just where you want it.

Items that can't go anywhere at the moment are greyed out. For example, elements need a section on the page first.

Hover over a section to see a larger preview of it.

## Finding something

- **Search** finds an item by its name or by what it's for: try "prices" or "map".
- **Favourites:** right-click any item and choose **Add to Favourites** to keep it at the top.
- **Recently used** lists the last few things you added.
- **Suggested** (Elements tab) lists what fits inside the selected item, such as another question for an FAQ or a column for Columns.
- **Shared sections** (Sections tab) lists the [sections you've shared](header-and-footer.md#shared-sections). Clicking one places another linked copy of it.

The Header and Footer aren't in the Add panel: every page already has them.

The Add panel is hidden in Content mode, which is for changing words and pictures only. See [The editor](the-editor.md#design-and-content-mode).

You can also add anything from **Search Commands…** (Ctrl+P): type "Add" and its name.
