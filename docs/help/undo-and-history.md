# Undo and Undo History

Every change you make to your site can be undone: text, pictures, pages, the theme, Site Settings. Each step has a name that says what changed, such as "Changed Heading size" or "Deleted page 'Events'".

- **Undo:** the **Undo** button in the title bar, or Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac).
- **Redo:** the **Redo** button, or Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y (Cmd+Shift+Z or Cmd+Y on a Mac).
- **Undo several steps:** the small arrow beside Undo lists the last ten steps. Choose one to undo it and every step after it.

After you delete something, an **Undo** button also appears for a few seconds.

## The Undo History panel

**Undo History**, beside the Add panel, lists your recent steps since you opened the site, up to the last 200, newest at the top. The step you're at is ticked; steps you've undone are greyed out. Click any step to go back, or forward, to that moment. {appName} also shows the page you were on and selects what you were working on.

Undo History lasts until you close the site. To go back further, use [Version History](version-history.md).

## Changes that reach other pages

Some changes reach every page, such as switching the theme, deleting a page that others link to, or giving every Button the same look. {appName} says how many pages will change and asks first. Each is still one step you can undo.
