# Templates and example sites

A **template** is a ready-made starting site: its pages, sections, sample text, pictures and a theme. You choose one when you make a site, and from then on the site is yours to change. A **theme** is something else: the site's colours, fonts and corners, which you can switch at any time (see [Themes](themes.md)).

| Template                 | Business type  | Theme    | Pages                                       |
| ------------------------ | -------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Café / Restaurant        | Food and drink | Bistro   | Home, Menu, About us, Visit us              |
| Salon / Beauty           | Beauty         | Atelier  | Home, Treatments, About us, Book            |
| Trades                   | Trades         | Workshop | Home, Services, Our work, About us, Contact |
| Consultant               | Services       | Meridian | Home, Services, About, Contact              |
| Photographer / Portfolio | Creative       | Darkroom | Home, Portfolio, About, Sessions, Contact   |
| Shop                     | Shop           | Market   | Home, Shop, About, Visit us                 |
| Personal / CV            | Personal       | Studio   | Home, About me, Work, Contact               |
| Blank site               |                | Light    | Home, About, Contact                        |

Every site also has a **Page not found** page, which visitors see when they follow a broken link.

The templates' photos are free to use, and they're copied into your site's [Media](media.md) when you make it. Replace them with your own whenever you like.

## Example sites

**Café / Restaurant** and **Trades** also come as finished examples, with complete sample content. **Try an example** on the start screen (or **Help › Try an Example** there) opens the Café / Restaurant one. In the quick start, choosing Food and drink or Trades offers that business's example. Open one to look around and try things. It's a site of your own, so nothing you change affects anything else.
