# Themes, colours and fonts

Your site's **theme** sets its colours, fonts, text sizes, buttons, corners and spacing on every page. Change it and the whole site follows, so everything stays consistent. Changes that affect every page ask you first, and each one is a single step you can undo.

You'll find the **Theme** panel in the right column, beside Site and Media, or under **View › Theme…**.

## Choosing a theme

The **Theme** list at the top of the Theme panel shows your own themes first, then the built-in ones:

- **Light**, **Dark** and **High contrast**.
- Twelve themes made for particular businesses:
  - **Bistro:** cafés, restaurants and bakeries.
  - **Atelier:** salons and spas.
  - **Workshop:** trades.
  - **Clarity** and **Meridian:** consultants and coaches.
  - **Darkroom:** photographers.
  - **Market:** shops and makers.
  - **Gazette:** boutiques and magazines.
  - **Harbour:** a dark theme.
  - **Linen:** florists and weddings.
  - **Studio:** CVs and designers.
  - **Playground:** children's activities and parties.

Switching theme changes the colours, fonts and styles on every page. Anything you've styled yourself with a theme colour picks up the new theme's version of that colour.

## Changing the theme

The Theme panel lists the theme's settings:

- **Colours:** **Brand**, **Accent**, **Second accent**, **Surface** (backgrounds) and **Text**. {appName} warns when two colours that sit together would be hard to read, and says how far off they are.
- **Fonts:**
  - **Font pairing:** a tested pair for headings and text, such as _Classic_, _Modern_ or _Friendly_.
  - Or choose **Headings**, **Body text** and **Accent** fonts yourself. The Accent font is for text whose Font is set to _Accent_.
  - Until you choose an Accent font of its own, it's the same as your Headings font and changes with it, and with a font pairing. Once you've chosen one, it stays.
- **Text sizes** for each heading level, body text and small text.
- **Headings:** capitals or small capitals, and letter spacing.
- **Buttons:** shape (_Square_, _Rounded_ or _Pill_), size and style.
- **Links:** underlined always, or only when pointed at.
- **Corners** of boxes, buttons and fields, from _Square_ to _Round_.
- **Photo corners**, **Card style**, **Section backgrounds** (all the same, or alternating), **Room around things**, **Shadows**, **Border width** and **Effects**.

## Built-in themes and your own

Built-in themes never change. The first time you change one, {appName} asks you to name **your own version** of it, such as "Bistro (my version)". Your own themes are kept on this computer, under **My themes**, for all your sites. Each site also keeps a copy of its theme, so it looks the same even if you delete your theme or open the site on another computer.

## Theme Studio

**Open Theme Studio**, at the top of the Theme panel (or **View › Theme Studio…**), shows the whole theme in a window that fills {appName}'s window. Its parts are tabs at the top:

- **Themes:** every theme drawn in its own colours and fonts, to compare. Create a **New theme…** from any theme, and rename or delete your own.
- **Colours:** every colour and its shades, for the page and for reversed sections side by side. **Colours from an image…** suggests brand colours from your logo or a photo in Media.
- **Text:** size, weight and line spacing for each kind of text.
- **Styles:** corners, shadows and the other settings, drawn as examples.
- **Components:** the look of every kind of section and element across the site, such as every Button or every card. Choose which kind from the list beside the tabs.

Above Colours, Text and Styles, a sample page shows your theme on Desktop and Mobile as you change it. It stays in view while you scroll the settings. To give it more room, drag the line under it, or select the line and use the arrow keys.

In **Components**, the kind you chose is shown in the same place. Your changes stay a draft until you click **Apply Changes** in the bar at the bottom, or **Discard** them. **Theme now** and **With your changes** above the preview compare the look before and after.

## Fonts

Choosing a font opens **Choose a font**, which shows each font written in itself. Type your own sample text to try them.

Fifty fonts come with {appName} and work without the internet. **Browse all fonts** lists many more, which download when you choose one and then work offline too. Only fonts that are free to use on websites are offered. Your website carries its own copy of its fonts, so it never loads them from elsewhere.

You can't add font files of your own yet.
