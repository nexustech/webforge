# Themes, colours and fonts

Your site's **theme** sets its colours, fonts, text sizes, buttons, corners and spacing on every page. Change it and the whole site follows, so everything stays consistent. Each change is a single step you can undo.

A new colour or font shows on your pages straight away, so you can try several. A message says how many pages it changed, such as "Brand colour changed on 6 pages", with **Undo** to take it back. Switching to another theme, or giving every item of one kind the same look, asks you first.

You'll find the **Theme** panel in the right column, beside Site and Media, or under **Site › Theme…**.

## Choosing a theme

The **Theme** list at the top of the Theme panel shows your own themes first, then the built-in ones:

- **Light**, **Dark** and **High contrast**.
- Twelve themes made for particular businesses:
  - **Bistro:** cafés, restaurants and bakeries.
  - **Atelier:** salons and spas.
  - **Workshop:** trades.
  - **Clarity** and **Meridian:** consultants and coaches.
  - **Darkroom:** photographers.
  - **Market:** shops and makers.
  - **Gazette:** boutiques and magazines.
  - **Harbour:** a dark theme.
  - **Linen:** florists and weddings.
  - **Studio:** CVs and designers.
  - **Playground:** children's activities and parties.

Switching theme changes the colours, fonts and styles on every page. Anything you've styled yourself with a theme colour picks up the new theme's version of that colour.

## Changing the theme

The Theme panel lists the theme's settings:

- **Colours:** **Brand**, **Accent**, **Second accent**, **Surface** (backgrounds) and **Text**. Click a colour's swatch to choose another in the colour picker (see [The colour picker](#the-colour-picker)).
  - When a colour you choose would be hard to read with one it sits with, {appName} says so under it straight away, such as "Brand is hard to read on Surface". Site Check says the same.
  - **Use a darker shade** (or **Use a lighter shade**) picks the nearest shade of the same colour that reads well.
- **Fonts:**
  - **Font pairing:** a tested pair for headings and text, such as _Classic_, _Modern_ or _Friendly_.
  - Or choose **Headings**, **Body text** and **Accent** fonts yourself. The Accent font is for text whose Font is set to _Accent_.
  - Until you choose an Accent font of its own, it's the same as your Headings font and changes with it, and with a font pairing. Once you've chosen one, it stays.
- **Text size:** **Smaller**, **Standard** or **Larger**, for all the text on your site at once. **Standard** is the size your theme comes with. It says **Your own** when you've sized kinds of text one by one.
- **Headings:** capitals or small capitals, and letter spacing.
- **Buttons:** shape (_Square_, _Rounded_ or _Pill_), size and style.
- **Links:** underlined always, or only when pointed at.
- **Corners** of boxes, buttons and fields, from _Square_ to _Round_.
- **Photo corners**, **Card style**, **Section backgrounds** (all the same, or alternating), **Room around things**, **Shadows**, **Border width** and **Effects**.

### The colour picker

Clicking a colour's swatch opens {appName}'s colour picker, the same on Windows and on a Mac:

- Drag in the large square to make the colour paler or richer (left and right) and lighter or darker (up and down). Drag the strip under it to change the colour itself.
- Type a colour's code, such as #1a7f5a, in the box under them, and press Enter. If your brand colour has a code, this is the easiest way to use it, in Simple mode too. A code has 6 digits and letters from a to f; {appName} says so if what you typed isn't one.
- Click the eyedropper beside the code, then click a colour on your screen, such as your logo, to take it. Press Escape to stop without taking one.
- With the keyboard, the arrow keys move in the square and along the strip; hold Shift to move further.

While you drag, type or pick a theme colour, or one of [your colours](#your-colours), your pages show it straight away, on the canvas and in Theme Studio's sample page, so you can see it on your own site before you settle on it. The colour is chosen when the picker closes: click the swatch again, or anywhere outside it. Press Escape to close it and keep the colour you had: your pages show it again. Each colour you choose is one step you can undo, however long you dragged.

### In Advanced mode

In Advanced mode (**View › Use Advanced Mode**), the Theme panel also shows:

- each colour's code, such as #405f39, which you can type in;
- how far off a colour that's hard to read is, such as "4.19:1, needs 4.5:1";
- a size for each heading level, body text and small text, in place of **Text size**.

Theme Studio always shows them.

## Built-in themes and your own

Built-in themes never change. The first time you change one, {appName} saves **your own version** of it, named after it, such as "Bistro (yours)". To give it a name of your own, rename it in Theme Studio's **Themes**. Your own themes are kept on this computer, under **My themes**, for all your sites. Each site also keeps a copy of its theme, so it looks the same even if you delete your theme or open the site on another computer.

## Theme Studio

**Open Theme Studio**, at the top of the Theme panel (or **Site › Theme Studio…**), shows the whole theme in a window that fills {appName}'s window. Its parts are tabs at the top:

- **Themes:** every theme drawn in its own colours and fonts, to compare. Create a **New Theme…** from any theme, and rename or delete your own.
- **Colours:** every colour and its shades, for the page and for reversed sections side by side, and [your colours](#your-colours). **Colours from an Image…** suggests brand colours from your logo or a photo in Media.
- **Text:** size, weight and line spacing for each kind of text.
- **Styles:** corners, shadows and the other settings, drawn as examples.
- **Components:** the look of every kind of section and element across the site, such as every Button or every card. Choose which kind from the list beside the tabs. A gradient is added as in **Style This…**: choose its direction, and it starts as Brand to Accent ([Gradients](styling.md#gradients)).

Above Colours, Text and Styles, a sample page shows your theme on Desktop and Mobile as you change it. It stays in view while you scroll the settings. To give it more room, drag the line under it, or select the line and use the arrow keys.

In **Components**, the kind you chose is shown in the same place. Your changes stay a draft until you click **Apply Changes** in the bar at the bottom, or **Discard** them. **Theme now** and **With your changes** above the preview compare the look before and after.

### Your colours

Besides the theme's five colours, you can keep your business's own colours in the theme, such as the green of your shop sign, each with a name. They're listed under **Your colours** at the top of Theme Studio's **Colours** tab:

- **Add a Colour** adds one, named "Colour 1", "Colour 2" and so on, starting as your Brand colour. Click its swatch to choose its colour in the [colour picker](#the-colour-picker).
- Type a new name in its box and press Enter. Renaming a colour changes nothing on your pages: everything that uses it keeps using it.
- Once you have six or more, **Find a colour by name** narrows the list.
- A theme can have up to 200 of your colours.
- A colour your site already uses that isn't in your theme can be added from the Edit panel with **Add to Your Colours…**, and everything that uses it then uses your colour ([Colours](styling.md#colours)).

Your colours are in every colour picker on the Edit panel, under **Your colours**, with their shades and opacity, and either end of a gradient can be one ([Colours](styling.md#colours)). Change one in Theme Studio and everything that uses it changes too.

**Delete** removes a colour. If nothing uses it, it goes straight away. If something does, {appName} says how many times, and asks what to do with them:

- **Use That Colour** gives them another of your colours, chosen in the list, with the same shade and opacity.
- **Keep Each as It Looks** keeps each one in the colour it has now, as a colour of its own that doesn't change with the theme.

Adding, renaming, changing and deleting a colour are each one step you can undo. [AI apps](ai-apps.md) can use your colours too, but can't remove one your site still uses: delete it here, where you choose what its uses become.

### Colours outside your theme

Under your colours, **Colours outside your theme** lists every colour your site uses that isn't in your theme, such as one chosen with **Another colour…** ([Colours](styling.md#colours)), and how many times each is used. A setting that uses it on both Desktop and Mobile counts once. These colours stay the same when you change your theme.

- Click a place under a colour, such as _Heading on Home_, to close Theme Studio and select that element on the page. A place marked _(not on any page)_ is in a shared section that isn't on any page yet, or in a blog design with no posts, so there's nothing to show.
- **Add to Your Colours…** adds the colour to [your colours](#your-colours), named as you like, and everything that uses it uses your colour from then on, each at its own opacity.
- **Use ‹name› Instead** gives everything that uses it the nearest of your colours, each at its own opacity, and a message says how many pages it changed, with **Undo**. It's offered once you have colours of your own.

Each is one step you can undo. If your site uses only your theme's colours, the list says so.

## Fonts

Choosing a font opens **Choose a Font**, which shows each font written in itself. Type your own sample text to try them.

Fifty fonts come with {appName} and work without the internet. **Browse All Fonts** lists many more, which download when you choose one and then work offline too. Only fonts that are free to use on websites are offered. Your website carries its own copy of its fonts, so it never loads them from elsewhere.

You can't add font files of your own yet.
