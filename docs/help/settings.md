# Settings

**Settings** (**File › Settings…**, or Ctrl+, on Windows; on a Mac, **{appName} › Settings…** or Cmd+,) has two parts:

- **App:** how {appName} works for you, on this computer, for every site. Described below.
- **This site:** the open site's [Site Settings](site-settings.md).

Click **Save** to keep your changes.

## Interface

- **Appearance:** _Dark_ or _Light_. This is how {appName} itself looks; it never changes your website. **View › Use Light Appearance** switches it too.
- **Editing:** _Simple mode_ or _Advanced mode_. Advanced mode adds the Edit panel's Advanced tab and Custom Code. See [The editor](the-editor.md#simple-and-advanced-mode).
- **Show tips:** a short tip the first time you reach some parts of {appName}, such as the first time you select something or switch to Content mode. Each tip shows once. Turn it off to see no more tips.
- **Show the tour and tips again:** tick it and choose **Save** to start over. The tour is offered the next time a site opens, and each tip shows once more. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#the-tour).

## Devices

**Your devices** adds screen sizes of your own to the canvas's device list, such as your own phone or a large monitor. Give each a **Name**, a **Width in pixels** and a **Height in pixels**, between 320 and 2560. You can also add one from the canvas toolbar's **Add device…**.

## Version History

**Versions kept:** how many of the versions {appName} saves by itself to keep for each site, from 5 to 100 (20 at first). Versions you save yourself are always kept. See [Version History](version-history.md).

## Updates

**Get updates from:** _Stable_ or _Beta_. Beta gets each new version of {appName} first, to try it out; Stable gets it once it has gone well on Beta.

- Until {appName} 1.0, every new version is a Beta version, so {appName} gets its updates from Beta unless you choose Stable. From 1.0 it's Stable, unless you choose Beta.
- Going back from Beta to Stable never puts an older version on: {appName} waits for a Stable version newer than the one you have.

How updates arrive is in [Getting started](getting-started.md#updates). On a Mac, {appName} doesn't update itself yet, so this changes nothing there.

## Assistant

**Assistant Settings…** opens the [assistant](assistant.md)'s settings: turning it on or off, where its requests go, the model that answers and its monthly limit. What you change there happens at once, without **Save**. Settings stays open behind it, with any changes you haven't saved yet.

## AI apps

**Open AI apps…** opens [AI apps](ai-apps.md), where you let AI apps such as Claude work on your site. What you change there happens at once, without **Save**. Settings stays open behind it, with any changes you haven't saved yet.
