# One-page sites

A one-page site has everything on its home page, one section after another: what you do, your prices, your photos, how to get in touch. The menu at the top scrolls down to each section. It suits a café, a tradesperson, an event or anyone with not much to say: visitors see it all by scrolling, and nothing is more than a click away.

## Starting one

In the [quick start](getting-started.md#start-a-site-the-quick-start), the **Pages** step asks **How your site is laid out**. Choose **One page**.

The pages you keep ticked, the template's and any you added from **More pages for** your business, become sections of your home page, in the order listed. {appName} then:

- puts each page's sections after the home page's own, and names the first section of each after its page;
- keeps one big banner (Hero) at the top: the other pages' banners would repeat it;
- makes the menu a link to each section, named after its page;
- points the template's buttons and links at the sections, so _Get a quote_ scrolls to your contact details;
- keeps the Header at the top as visitors scroll, so the menu is always in reach.

Your site still has a **Page not found** page, for visitors who mistype an address.

Look through the page once it opens: delete any section you don't need, and move the others into the order you want. Every template works as one page.

## Adding a section to the menu

To link to a section you add later:

1. In the [Site panel's Menu](pages.md#the-menu), click **+** (**Add to Menu**) and choose **Link…**.
2. Under **Link to**, choose **Page**, then your home page, then the **Section**.
3. Name it, and click **Save Link**.

A button or text can link to a section the same way: see [Links](links.md).

## The Header that stays

**Stay at the top when scrolling** is on for a one-page site, so visitors can jump to any section from anywhere on the page. A section a link scrolls to stops just below the Header, never under it. You can turn it off in the Header's options: see [Header, Footer and shared sections](header-and-footer.md).

## More pages later

A one-page site can grow. Add a page in the Pages panel whenever you need one, such as a privacy page, and add it to the menu.
