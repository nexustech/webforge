Our story

Hannah opened The Copper Kettle in 2016, in what used to be the town's ironmonger. The old copper kettle on the counter came with the building.

We cook everything here, we buy from farms and bakers nearby, and we try to make every visit feel like coming round to a friend's for breakfast.
