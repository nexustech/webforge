Good to know

Do you take bookings?

For six or more, yes: call us the day before. Otherwise just come in, and we'll find you a seat.

Can I bring my dog?

Of course. Dogs on leads are welcome inside and out.

Is there step-free access?

Yes, through the front door, and there's an accessible toilet.

Do you do vegan food?

Yes. Ask for the vegan breakfast, or the soup, which is usually vegan too.

Questions about catering, parties or anything else. We reply within a day.
