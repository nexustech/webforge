About me

I'm a designer and illustrator working from a small studio by the river. I grew up drawing on everything, and I still start every project with a pencil.

I work best with people who care about what they make: bakers, makers, shop owners and small teams. I keep things simple, explain my choices, and stay in touch from the first call to the last file.
