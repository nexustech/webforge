Our salon

Ella opened Willow in 2018 after twelve years of working in city salons. She wanted somewhere calmer, where every appointment comes with a cup of tea and nobody is rushed.

We use cruelty-free products, recycle our foils and colour tubes, and keep learning: every one of us trains for at least a week a year.
