Our story

Fern & Clay started on a kitchen table in 2019, with one second-hand wheel and a lot of wonky pots.

Now we make everything in a small studio by the river: thrown, trimmed, glazed and fired by the two of us, a batch at a time.

The makers

Two people, one kiln and a lot of tea.

Mia

Potter

Tom

Glazes and packing
